# Materials And Alchemy

Documents clay, glaze, kiln, firing, and material experiments, with emphasis on process control, material behavior, craft decisions, and the transformation from raw material to finished work.

## Working Guide

Start with the [materials journal index](trade_journals/README.md). Scope an
update to one study, batch, historical record, or evidence section within the
relevant journal. Preserve the connections between making, firing, observation,
and the lessons carried into later work.

Use the [Flickr inventory](../FLICKR_PUBLIC_ALBUMS.md) for the primary pottery
album and the [Google Photos inventory](../GOOGLE_PHOTOS_ALBUMS.md) for its
secondary mirror. Keep each platform's source identities distinct. Clay-body
identification, firing state, critique, and study progress belong in the journal
with their supporting observations.

For an update:

1. Read the relevant entries and evidence needs before adding another checkpoint.
2. Update the bounded section and its image references together; retain the
   distinction between historical work and the current practice.
3. Update the journal index if navigation changes. Follow the
   [publishing guide](../site_example/README.md#portfolio-publishing) when the
   public study summary or representative images change.

Use the [existing validation checks](../MODULE_MAP.md#existing-validation).
Keep current study status in the journal rather than repeating it in this guide.

## Suggested Journal Topics

- clay bodies and forming methods
- glaze testing, recipes, and outcomes
- kiln building, maintenance, and firing logs
- surface experiments and material failures
- finished pieces and lessons learned
