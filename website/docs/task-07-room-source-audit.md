# Task 07 Three-Room Source Audit

Status: source audit for the private Task 07 draft. This document records the
evidence boundary for public copy. It does not correct the journals or album
inventories, approve content, publish a revision, or establish that the future
barre studio has entered service.

## Evidence Rule

Room ownership and occupancy follow Shawn's September 18 correction recorded
in the [Task 07 brief](../../docs/website-workflow/task-07-edit-project.md#confirmed-room-identities)
and the [approved editor design](../../docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-18-website-project-editor-design.md#authoritative-room-identities).
Album titles, photographs, and dates do not override that correction.

The audit keeps four evidence classes separate:

1. **Confirmed ownership** identifies the physical room and its current or
   future use.
2. **Direct observation** states only what is visible in a selected image.
3. **Album metadata** records the saved inventory description, count, and date
   range without turning those fields into room-history claims.
4. **Journal claims** remain source assertions that need room reassignment or
   correction before reuse.
