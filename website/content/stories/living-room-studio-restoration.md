# Barre Studio — Restored Living Room

The former living room in Shawn and Haley’s 1894 residence is now home to the barre studio and Repair Shop. Original floorboards, exposed shiplap, reclaimed ceiling repairs, mineral finishes and fitted trim remain central to the work.

Restoration was completed in August/September 2026. Decorating, furnishing and preparation for the new studio followed, leading to the grand reopening and student appreciation event on November 7, 2026.

The selected photographs move from the room’s starting condition and hand preparation of the floor to its finished, furnished use. The November views show the restored woodwork alongside the barre, mirrors and personal touches. The event date comes from Shawn’s account; the room photographs do not document attendance or an exact move day.
