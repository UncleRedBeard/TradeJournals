# Living Room Restoration — Future Barre Studio

The former living room in the 1894 residence is being restored as the future home of the barre studio and Repair Shop. Original floorboards, exposed shiplap, reclaimed ceiling repairs, mineral finishes, and fitted trim remain central to the work.

The source journal documents renovation through August 30, 2026. The move has not been confirmed, so this room remains the future studio and must not be described as current.
