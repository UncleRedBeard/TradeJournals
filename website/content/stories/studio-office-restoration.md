# Barre Studio — Current Room

This room is the current home of the barre studio and Repair Shop. The selected photographs come from the Home Reno - Studio | Office Flickr album.

An original solid-wood door left with the house was worn but worth keeping. Shawn and Haley stripped it to bare wood rather than replacing it. The project notes describe a torch-developed charred appearance, chosen for material reclamation and surface character. This was an adaptation for appearance, not a claim of traditional yakisugi or fire resistance.

The stripped door appears in the room before that separate treatment. Two later detail photographs show finish being brushed across the dark textured wood and the checked char surface close up. The notes record one coat of water-based polyurethane followed by a custom Dutch-oil mixture; they do not establish the exact products, mixture ratios or drying intervals.

The journal's 2022 door heading conflicts with mostly February 2023 Flickr date labels, so these images are grouped by what they show rather than used as a dated sequence. The record does not show that the charred door was finally installed. The room remains the current barre studio and Repair Shop home until the move to the former living room is confirmed.
