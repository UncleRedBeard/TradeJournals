# Dedicated Office

This room is the dedicated office in the 1894 residence. The selected photographs come from the Home Reno - Office album and show the room, retained flooring, trim, windows, and office use.

One close photograph documents a small access opening above the baseboard: a receptacle sits outside the wall, with nearby floor protection and tape. It makes a localized intervention visible without turning it into a room-wide demolition story. The photograph does not explain the hidden wiring decisions or establish the full electrical scope.

The source journal now separates the current Barre Studio's floor and retained-door work from this dedicated Office. Those processes belong to the studio, not this room. The Office floor chronology remains unverified; the room-use photographs and the access detail are not presented as a dated construction sequence.
