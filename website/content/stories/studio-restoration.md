# A New Purpose For An Old Room

The former living room in the author's 1894 home is being restored as a
ballet barre studio. The work reaches from floor to ceiling, but its character
comes from what was kept: the original floorboards, the shiplap uncovered beneath
drywall, and a plank ceiling repaired with locally sourced reclaimed wood.

The floor needed hand scraping before progressive sanding and hand-brushed
polyurethane. Once refinished, it was covered with builder's paper so the wall
and ceiling work could continue without undoing that effort. Keeping the floor
meant planning the order of the work as carefully as the finish itself.

The shiplap was sanded and left exposed. Around it, white lime paint and lime
slurry brought the walls and ceiling together, while replacement crown molding
required coping and careful fitting. The aim was a room that felt whole, with
its old materials still visible and useful.

By late August, the floor covering was lifted and the repaired ceiling, mineral
finishes, exposed wood, and trim could be seen together. The source journal
still records finishing work in progress through August 30; it does not yet
document final sign-off or the installed ballet barre.
