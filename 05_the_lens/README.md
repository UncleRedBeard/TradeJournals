# The Lens

Documents a lifelong photography practice that began in childhood through family influence, developed through formal black-and-white study, and continues across film and digital formats.

Photography is a supporting practice within TradeJournals rather than a primary trade offering. It is both an independent creative outlet and the visual language used to make restoration, material, machine, and pottery work legible. Personal narrative and curated bodies of work connect the archive, while Flickr and Lomography records remain separate so their source metadata and provenance stay intact.

## Working Guide

Start with the [lens journal index](trade_journals/README.md). Choose one camera,
photographic collection, or platform record. Use
[Learning to See](trade_journals/learning_to_see.md) for the practice's broader
narrative and the individual catalogs for their source evidence.

Resolve Flickr albums through the [shared inventory](../FLICKR_PUBLIC_ALBUMS.md).
Keep related Flickr and Lomography records separate even when images overlap.
Before changing the [France archive](trade_journals/flickr_france_travel_archive.md),
check its relationship to the standalone Toulon, La Turbie, and
Roqueburne-Cap-Martin entries listed in the journal index. Identify which record
owns the addition and review shared references for consistency.

For an update:

1. Read the selected narrative or catalog and identify the source platform.
2. Preserve source labels and uncertainty about camera, film, capture date, or
   scan metadata. Keep interpretation and review progress in the journal.
3. Update the index if navigation changes. Use the
   [publishing guide](../site_example/README.md#portfolio-publishing) when
   changing a selected body of work or its representative images.

Photographs documenting a restoration remain evidence for that craft journal;
their use does not require moving the project into the lens area. Use the
[existing validation checks](../MODULE_MAP.md#existing-validation).

## Suggested Journal Topics

- family photographic lineage and inherited cameras
- early photographs, physical albums, and darkroom education
- film cameras, lenses, and stocks used for documentation
- scanning, archiving, and metadata practices
- Flickr album organization and image descriptions
- before, during, and after photo sequencing
- visual standards for future portfolio evidence
