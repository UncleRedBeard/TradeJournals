## Editorial Result And Limits

The pending marker is replaced with a bounded account of localized electrical
access. The empty `Demolition and opening` key-photo line now points to photo
`53718846780` and explicitly rejects a room-scale demolition inference. The
photographs do not show when or why these openings were made, what was removed,
or any concealed framing or wall condition. A specific demolition method, extent,
or preservation choice cannot be written from this evidence alone. If Shawn has
work notes or earlier images of removal and discoveries, those would be needed
before a fuller demolition narrative is supportable.

The Office journal remains a mixed source: its 2021 floor-refinishing and door
entries, many key-photo lines, and several visual-evidence categories still cite
the Studio | Office album for the dedicated Office. The source audit identifies
this contradiction. Correcting or reassigning those sections is separate work;
this task did not silently alter them.
