# The Residence: 1894 Historic Home Restoration

Documents restoration work on the 1894 residence, with emphasis on historic preservation, structural stabilization, old-growth materials, and repair decisions that protect original building fabric.

## Working Guide

Start with the [residence journal index](trade_journals/README.md). Scope work to
one room, system, or intervention, and keep its condition, decisions, progress,
and missing evidence in that journal.

Consult the [Flickr inventory](../FLICKR_PUBLIC_ALBUMS.md) for album routing and
recorded counts. The ballet studio's shared-album record is in the
[Google Photos inventory](../GOOGLE_PHOTOS_ALBUMS.md). Keep the ballet studio
distinct from the older Studio | Office Flickr album, which belongs to the
office journal. The [sketchbook guide](../source_materials/scanned_sketchbooks/README.md)
maps shared source pages to their rooms; preserve the complete scan.

For an update:

1. Read the selected journal's dated entries and visual-evidence sections.
2. Add the supported work record and what the evidence proves. Preserve
   distinctions between work in progress and confirmed room-ready results.
3. Update the journal index if navigation changes. If a portfolio claim or
   representative selection changes, follow the
   [publishing guide](../site_example/README.md#portfolio-publishing).

Use the [existing validation checks](../MODULE_MAP.md#existing-validation).
Keep this guide as navigation; project status belongs in the room journal.

## Suggested Journal Topics

- structural framing and sill stabilization
- foundation, piers, leveling, and deflection correction
- finish carpentry and millwork replication
- historic windows, sash repair, glazing, and wavy glass
- exterior envelope, siding, drainage, and weather protection
- modern system integration inside historic framing
